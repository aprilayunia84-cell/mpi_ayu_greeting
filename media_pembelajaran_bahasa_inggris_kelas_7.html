Greetings (Salam Perjumpaan)

Ungkapan yang digunakan saat bertemu dengan seseorang.

Topik 01
Klik ikon pada tabel untuk mendengarkan pengucapan kata dalam bahasa Inggris!
Greeting (Ungkapan) Response (Jawaban) Konteks / Waktu Suara
Good morning Good morning Selamat pagi (00.00 - 12.00)
Good afternoon Good afternoon Selamat siang/sore (12.00 - 18.00)
Good evening Good evening Selamat malam (18.00 - menjelang tidur)
How are you? I am fine, thank you / Very well Menanyakan kabar
How do you do? How do you do? Salam pertama kali bertemu (Formal)

Contoh Dialog Singkat:

Siti: "Hello Edo, good morning!"

Edo: "Good morning Siti, how are you?"

Siti: "I am fine, thanks. How about you?"

Edo: "I am great! Ready for our space class!"

Self Introduction (Perkenalan Diri)

Pola kalimat untuk memperkenalkan diri sendiri secara lengkap.

Topik 03

Unsur Informasi Penting:

  • Greeting: Hello / Hi / Good morning
  • Name: My name is... / I am...
  • Age: I am 12 years old.
  • Origin/Address: I live in Jakarta. / I am from Indonesia.
  • Hobby: My hobby is reading books.
Teks Kartu

Contoh Perkenalan Diri:

"Hello everyone! Let me introduce myself. My name is Galang. I am 13 years old. I live in South Tangerang. My hobby is playing football. Nice to meet you all!"

Introducing Others (Mengenalkan Orang Lain)

Cara mengenalkan teman atau orang lain kepada seseorang.

Topik 04

Ungkapan Utama:

"This is my friend, [Nama]"

Ini adalah temanku, [Nama]

"I'd like you to meet [Nama]"

Saya ingin mengenalkanmu pada [Nama]

Ingat Kata Ganti Pronouns!

Laki-laki (Boy): Subject: He
Possessive: His name...
Perempuan (Girl): Subject: She
Possessive: Her name...

Contoh Mengenalkan Teman:

Bima: "Hi Andre, this is my classmate, Monita. She is from Bali."
Andre: "Hi Monita, nice to meet you!"
Monita: "Nice to meet you too, Andre!"
